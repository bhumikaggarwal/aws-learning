# Vulnerabilities to Cloud Computing

Cloud environments face security threats due to shared infrastructure, internet exposure, and misconfigurations.

---

## 1. Data Breaches
- Unauthorized access to sensitive data.
- Caused by weak security, poor access control, or cyberattacks.

**Mitigation:** Encryption, IAM, MFA.

---

## 2. Data Loss
- Permanent loss of data due to accidental deletion, hardware failure, or ransomware.

**Mitigation:** Regular backups and disaster recovery plans.

---

## 3. Misconfiguration
- Incorrect cloud settings expose resources to the public.
- One of the most common cloud security risks.

**Example:** Publicly accessible cloud storage bucket.

---

## 4. Insecure APIs
- Weak or vulnerable APIs can be exploited by attackers.
- APIs are the primary way cloud services communicate.

**Mitigation:** Authentication, authorization, API rate limiting.

---

## 5. Account Hijacking
- Attackers steal user credentials and gain unauthorized access.
- Common methods include phishing and credential theft.

**Mitigation:** MFA, strong passwords, least privilege access.

---

## 6. Insider Threats
- Employees or contractors intentionally or accidentally compromise data.
- Can result in data leaks or service disruption.

---

## 7. DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service)
- Attackers flood cloud services with excessive traffic.
- Makes applications slow or unavailable.

**Mitigation:** DDoS protection services, load balancers, CDNs.

---

## 8. Malware & Ransomware
- Malicious software infects cloud workloads.
- Ransomware encrypts data and demands payment.

**Mitigation:** Endpoint protection, backups, regular updates.

---

## 9. Shared Technology Vulnerabilities
- Cloud customers share physical infrastructure.
- Weaknesses in virtualization or hypervisors may affect multiple tenants.

---

## 10. Compliance & Regulatory Risks
- Failure to comply with industry regulations can lead to legal and financial penalties.
- Examples: GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS.

---
